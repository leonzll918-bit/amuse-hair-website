About Amuse

A thoughtful approach to hair.

Amuse is a modern hair salon in Kuala Lumpur, focused on thoughtful colour, cuts, styling and treatments.

How we work

Listen first.
Then create.

We begin by understanding your hair, the styles you feel drawn to, your day-to-day and the result you have in mind. Then we talk through an approach that suits you.

A clear conversation helps us make thoughtful recommendations, together.

What we focus on

Care in every detail.

  1. 01

    Colour

    Thoughtful hair colour is a core part of Amuse. We consider your hair and the look you have in mind before recommending a direction.

  2. 02

    Technique

    Professional technical work across cutting, styling, perms, rebonding and treatments, shaped around the result you want.

  3. 03

    Service

    Friendly communication and a comfortable, straightforward experience—so it feels easy to talk through your options.
