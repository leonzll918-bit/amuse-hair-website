Hair Colour FAQ

Do I need to bleach my hair for my desired colour?

It depends on your desired colour, current base colour, previous colour and hair condition. Share photos of your current hair and target colour so we can advise on the likely approach.

Can I colour previously bleached or damaged hair?

We need to assess your current hair condition and previous chemical treatments before advising whether colouring is suitable. We cannot guarantee that it will be suitable before an assessment.

What's the difference between balayage and highlights?

Balayage creates softer, blended dimension with a customised, hand-painted effect. Highlights lighten selected sections for more defined brightness or contrast. The result also depends on placement.

How long does a hair colour appointment take?

Appointment time depends on your current hair, colour history, technique and whether bleaching is required. Contact us for an estimate before your visit.

How much does hair colour cost?

Hair colour pricing depends on your current hair, hair length, technique, whether bleaching is needed and the result you want. Send us your current hair photo and desired colour on WhatsApp for a personalised consultation.
