# Amuse Hair Studio — Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-10-02

## Business

**Business name:** Amuse Hair Studio  
**Business type:** Local hair salon  
**Primary location:** OUG, Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia  
**Nearby commercially relevant areas:** Old Klang Road, Bukit Jalil, Sri Petaling, Kuchai Lama, and nearby Kuala Lumpur areas within practical travelling distance.  
**Business model:** Appointment-driven local service business.

**Primary digital conversion:** Qualified WhatsApp conversation.  
**Primary business conversion:** Confirmed booking and completed salon visit.

**Customer journey:**  
Traffic → Interest → Trust → WhatsApp → Booking → Salon Visit → Repeat Visit → Revenue

## Current Commercial Priority

Use this current service priority unless reliable performance data suggests otherwise:

1. Hair Colour
2. Hair Treatment
3. Haircut
4. Perm

Do not treat this ordering as immutable. When enough data exists, marketing allocation should also consider search demand, customer demand, average ticket, conversion rate, repeat potential, upsell potential, customer acquisition potential, CAC, ROAS, operational capacity, and revenue contribution.

Historical sales alone should not determine future marketing priority.

## Important Services

### Hair Colour

Current primary growth category.

Useful marketing themes include Before & After, transformation, stylist expertise, consultation, colour suitability, hair condition, trust, and result quality. Avoid unsupported claims.

### Keratin Treatment

**Starting price:** RM199  
**Important differentiator:** Formaldehyde-free.

Primary benefits:
- Reduce frizz
- Improve manageability
- Smoother-looking hair
- Easier daily styling

Do not describe Keratin Treatment as permanent straightening.

### Bio Treatment

**Starting price:** RM280

Positioning: approximately 90% treatment + 10% chemical process.

Primary use: reduce frizz while maintaining a relatively natural result.

### Rebonding

**Starting price:** RM280

Positioning: chemical straightening designed for a straighter and longer-lasting result.

**Taxonomy rule:** Do not classify Rebonding under Hair Treatment. Use Perm / Chemical Texture Services.

## Positioning

Preferred positioning:

**Reliable, approachable, professional neighborhood salon with strong results at reasonable mid-market pricing.**

Do not position Amuse as:
- ultra-budget
- discount-only
- ultra-luxury

Prefer evidence, proof, clarity, and customer outcomes over exaggerated marketing language.

## Local SEO

For local search decisions prioritize:

**Local Intent × Commercial Intent × Relevance × Conversion Potential**

Relevant query patterns may include:
- hair salon OUG
- hair colour OUG
- hair treatment OUG
- keratin treatment KL
- hair salon near me

Do not create thin doorway pages solely to target neighboring areas.

Local SEO analysis should consider Google Business Profile, Map Pack visibility, NAP consistency, reviews, services, categories, website relevance, internal linking, schema, local competitors, search intent, and conversion to WhatsApp.

## Measurement

Marketing analysis should distinguish:

**DATA FACT** — directly demonstrated by available data.  
**INTERPRETATION** — a reasoned reading of the data.  
**HYPOTHESIS** — requires testing or additional evidence.  
**UNKNOWN** — evidence is unavailable.

Never present hypotheses as facts.

Primary performance metrics:
- Qualified WhatsApp conversations
- Bookings
- Completed customers
- Revenue
- CPA / CAC
- ROAS
- Conversion rate
- Average ticket
- Repeat rate
- Customer lifetime value

Secondary metrics:
- Traffic
- Impressions
- Reach
- Views
- Followers
- Engagement

Do not optimize primarily for vanity metrics.

## Decision Principle

The objective of marketing is not maximum traffic.

The objective is profitable customer acquisition and long-term customer value.

Whenever possible connect recommendations to:

Attention → Interest → Trust → WhatsApp → Booking → Customer → Repeat → Revenue
